Research Agenda

Three Pillars of Open Computational Work

Every project listed here uses publicly accessible inputs, versioned code, and reproducible analytical archives built for independent verification.

01

Genomics & Functional Evidence Calibration

Auditing how well published candidate-variant associations and cancer gene-dependency claims hold up against genome-wide GWAS Catalog, ClinVar, and DepMap CRISPR/RNAi perturbation screens, alongside detecting flanking 129-strain donor DNA directly from knockout mouse RNA-seq reads.

variant-claim-atlas depclaims flank129
02

Biobank Genetic Architecture & Cell-State Mapping

Applying genomic structural equation modelling, Mendelian randomisation, LD score regression, single-cell genetic-load mapping (scDRS/MAGMA), and chromatin heritability partitioning across FinnGen, UK Biobank, and synovial/tendon single-cell atlases.

hand-shoulder-genetic-axes ra-synovial-stroma-genetics diagnostic-codes
03

Clinical Pharmacology & Randomized Trial Safety

Building temporally validated hierarchical trial-arm models linking ClinicalTrials.gov adverse event tables to US FDA prescription labels (openFDA, SIDER, ChEMBL) and testing drug-repositioning hypotheses across hundreds of placebo-controlled trials.

placebo-adjusted-labels afstd 881-RCT meta-analysis

Variant–Trait Claim Atlas

Zero-tracking browser tool and Zenodo archive calibrating 201,720 published variant–trait claims from 71,577 PubMed abstracts against NHGRI-EBI GWAS Catalog evidence and 239 ClinVar risk variants, with Python and R clients.

python examples/vca_client.py check rs7903146 "type 2 diabetes"

RNA-seq CLI GRCm38/39 · MIT

flank129

Calls residual 129-strain donor DNA flanking a targeted gene directly from a knockout mouse study's own RNA-seq reads using 4.46M shared SNP sites (plus CBA/J, DBA/2J, BALB/cJ, C3H/HeJ, and private panels) to flag flanking confounding.

flank129.py call example/samples_GSE173144.tsv Mus_musculus.GRCm38.102.gtf.gz Cxcr5 out/
GitHub Repository ↗
Python Package DepMap 26Q1 · MIT

depclaims

Installable Python CLI and library plus CC BY 4.0 atlas scoring 13,610 published cancer gene-dependency claims (17,920 statements across 18,530 genes) against DepMap 26Q1 CRISPR (Chronos) and DEMETER2 RNAi screens.

depclaims score --cache depmap_cache KRAS PANC-1
